# Covered Services and Exclusions

**Document ID:** CH-BN-005
**Effective date:** 1 January 2025
**Owner:** Benefit Configuration

> Fictional document created for a software demonstration.

---

## 1. How to read this document

This document states **what is covered** and **what is excluded**. It does not state dollar amounts: deductibles, copays, coinsurance rates and out-of-pocket maximums live in the plan benefit records, which are the authoritative source for any figure.

A service must clear three tests to be paid:

1. It is a covered benefit under this document
2. It is medically necessary for the member's condition
3. Any applicable prior authorization is on file (see CH-UM-004)

Failing test 1 produces **DN-NOT-COVERED**, which is a contractual exclusion and rarely overturned on appeal. Failing test 2 produces **DN-MED-NEC**, which is clinical and frequently overturned with adequate documentation. The distinction matters: appealing an exclusion as though it were a medical necessity denial wastes the 180-day window.

## 2. Covered benefit categories

### Preventive and wellness
Annual preventive visit, immunisations, and the screenings listed in the Member Handbook, covered at no cost share in network and before the deductible.

### Primary and specialist care
Office visits, consultations, second opinions, and telehealth. Telehealth is covered at the same cost share as the equivalent in-person visit for the same service.

### Diagnostic services
Laboratory, pathology, plain radiography, ultrasound, and advanced imaging. Advanced imaging requires prior authorization outside the emergency department.

### Emergency and urgent care
Emergency department care, emergency ambulance transport, urgent care centres, and post-stabilisation care. Always covered at the in-network level (CH-NW-003 Section 6).

### Hospital and surgical
Inpatient medical and surgical admissions, outpatient surgery, ambulatory surgical centre procedures, anaesthesia, and implanted devices and prosthetics that are integral to a covered procedure.

### Maternity and newborn
Prenatal and postnatal care, delivery, and newborn care. Prenatal visits are covered at no cost share as preventive care. A newborn is covered for the first 31 days under the parent's coverage; enrolment must be completed within that window for coverage to continue.

### Behavioural health and substance use
Outpatient therapy, psychiatric medication management, intensive outpatient and partial hospitalisation, inpatient psychiatric care, and medication-assisted treatment for substance use disorder. **Cost sharing for behavioural health is no greater than for the equivalent medical or surgical benefit**, and no visit limits are applied to behavioural health that are not applied to medical care.

### Rehabilitative and habilitative therapy
Physical, occupational and speech therapy. The first 12 visits per discipline per plan year require no authorization; beyond that, authorization is required and continued coverage depends on documented functional progress.

### Home health and skilled nursing
Intermittent skilled nursing, home infusion, and skilled nursing facility care following a qualifying hospital stay. Both require authorization beyond the initial thresholds in CH-UM-004.

### Durable medical equipment
Equipment that is medically necessary, primarily serves a medical purpose, and is appropriate for home use. Repairs and one replacement per five years for equipment still medically necessary. Items above $1,000 and rentals beyond 60 days require authorization.

### Hospice and palliative care
Full hospice benefit for members with a terminal prognosis, including respite care and bereavement support for the family.

### Prescription drugs
Covered under the pharmacy benefit; see CH-RX-006.

### Dental and vision
- **Paediatric** dental and vision are covered as essential health benefits for members under 19, including one exam and one set of corrective lenses per plan year.
- **Adult** dental and routine adult vision are **not** covered. See the exclusions below.

## 3. Exclusions

The following are excluded from all Cascade plans. A claim for any of these is denied **DN-NOT-COVERED**, and the member may be billed.

### Cosmetic and elective
- Cosmetic surgery and procedures performed to improve appearance without restoring function
- Hair removal, hair transplantation and hair-loss treatment
- Skin treatments for cosmetic purposes, including chemical peels and dermabrasion
- Tattoo removal, except where removing a tattoo is required for a covered procedure

**Reconstructive surgery is not cosmetic and is covered** where it restores function or corrects a deformity resulting from congenital anomaly, injury, or a covered procedure — including breast reconstruction following mastectomy.

### Not medically established
- Services that are experimental or investigational, except in an approved clinical trial for a life-threatening condition
- Treatments not recognised as effective by the relevant specialty consensus
- Off-label drug use, unless supported by a recognised compendium

### Adult dental and vision
- Routine adult dental care, including exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns and extractions
- Adult orthodontia
- Routine adult eye exams for refraction, eyeglasses and contact lenses
- Refractive surgery, including LASIK

**Exception:** dental care is covered when it is an integral part of treating a covered medical condition — for example, dental extraction required before cardiac valve surgery or before head and neck radiotherapy, or repair of the jaw or teeth following an accidental injury. Vision care is covered for medical eye disease such as glaucoma, cataract, retinal disease and eye injury; only *routine refraction* is excluded.

### Convenience and non-medical
- Personal comfort items, including television, telephone and private room charges above the semi-private rate
- Custodial and long-term care where no skilled need exists
- Homemaker and companion services
- Gym memberships, exercise equipment and general fitness programmes
- Nutritional supplements, vitamins and food, except enteral formula that is the sole source of nutrition
- Travel and lodging, except where a gap exception explicitly includes it

### Reproductive and family building
- Assisted reproductive technology, including in-vitro fertilisation, and donor services
- Surrogacy-related services
- Reversal of voluntary sterilisation

**Covered:** diagnostic evaluation of infertility, contraception including all methods approved as preventive care, and voluntary sterilisation.

### Other exclusions
- Services provided by an immediate family member
- Services covered by workers' compensation, or by a third party's liability
- Care furnished by a government facility at no charge to the member
- Missed-appointment fees, form-completion fees and record-copying charges
- Services delivered outside the United States, except emergency care during temporary travel
- Court-ordered treatment that is not otherwise medically necessary
- Weight-loss programmes, other than bariatric surgery meeting the criteria below

## 4. Benefits with specific criteria

### Bariatric surgery
Covered once per lifetime when the member meets all of: BMI ≥ 40, or ≥ 35 with a qualifying comorbidity; documented participation in a supervised weight-management programme for at least six months; a completed behavioural health evaluation; and age 18 or over. Prior authorization is required. Revision surgery is covered only for a surgical complication, not for inadequate weight loss.

### Gender-affirming care
Covered as medically necessary care consistent with recognised clinical standards, including hormone therapy, surgical treatment, and associated behavioural health support. Prior authorization is required for surgical treatment. **These services are not subject to the cosmetic exclusion.**

### Genetic testing
Covered where the result will change clinical management, and for the screening panels recognised as preventive care. Direct-to-consumer ancestry and wellness genomic testing is excluded. Panels not listed as preventive require prior authorization.

### Clinical trials
Routine patient care costs associated with participation in an approved clinical trial for a life-threatening condition are covered. The investigational item or service itself, and data-collection costs, are not.

### Transplants
Covered at designated centres of excellence, with prior authorization. Donor evaluation and procurement are covered as part of the recipient's benefit.

## 5. When coverage and necessity conflict

A service can be a covered benefit and still be denied as not medically necessary — for example, MRI is a covered diagnostic service, but an MRI for uncomplicated low back pain within six weeks of onset and without red-flag findings does not meet clinical criteria and is denied **DN-MED-NEC**.

Conversely, a service can be medically necessary and still excluded — adult dental restoration may be entirely necessary and remain outside the benefit.

Read the denial code carefully before responding. The two denials require completely different evidence, and CH-AG-002 Section 4 sets out what each one needs.
